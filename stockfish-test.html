Chess Move Classification Tester v1.12.3.0

Diagnostic build comparing Stockfish's best move and the played move from the same pre-move position.

Move and game accuracy now follow the supplied Lichess AccuracyPercent.scala structure. Sequential position evaluations are converted to Win%, move accuracy is calculated from the reduction in the mover's Win%, and game accuracy is the mean of the volatility-weighted mean and harmonic mean.

Best is reserved for an exact match with Stockfish's reported best move. Book moves remain labelled Book but still receive calculated move accuracy. Mate scores are converted to plus or minus 1,000 centipawns for the Lichess game-accuracy calculation. Mate distance is therefore not separately penalised once forced mate exists.
