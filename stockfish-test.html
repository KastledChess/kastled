Chess Move Classification Tester v1.14.1

Diagnostic dual-model build. Stockfish analysis is run once. The page then shows the published Lichess model and a provisional Chess.com-calibration candidate side by side.

Lichess model: sequential position Win%, uncertainty bonus, volatility-weighted mean and harmonic mean. Candidate model: root WDL expected-points loss, a small bounded mate-distance adjustment, and a game blend of 35% arithmetic mean, 45% harmonic mean and 20% capped critical-position mean.

The candidate constants are deliberately exposed for testing. Great, Brilliant and Miss are not fabricated in this build. Book and Forced are contextual labels. Best requires an exact Stockfish bestmove match.
